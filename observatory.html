About SPHEREx

Discovering our Universe

We believe that looking at the stars is more than simply observing distant objects. It is an opportunity to understand where we came from, how the universe works, and what mysteries remain unexplored. Our observatory provides opportunities for learning, observation, research, and public engagement with astronomy.
