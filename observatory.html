Who we are

NightLock is an interactive astronomy platform inspired by NASA’s SPHEREx mission, designed to make infrared astronomy and space science accessible through immersive visualisations and interactive learning. It helps students, researchers and space enthusiasts explore the universe, understand scientific discoveries and uncover the origins of galaxies, stars and planetary systems.

About SPHEREx

Discovering the Hidden Universe

NASA's SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer) is a space-based infrared observatory designed to conduct an unprecedented all-sky spectral survey. By observing the universe in 102 different infrared wavelength bands, SPHEREx helps scientists investigate the origins and evolution of galaxies, study the history of cosmic structures and identify water and organic molecules in regions where stars and planetary systems form. Through NightLock, we bring these scientific concepts closer to everyone with interactive visualisations, educational resources and an accessible digital exploration experience.
