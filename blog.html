Explore the SPHEREx Universe

SPHEREx observes the universe using 102 different infrared wavelength bands. Discover how infrared light reveals details about stars, galaxies, and cosmic dust that human eyes cannot see.

NASA's SPHEREx mission is creating a detailed spectral map of the entire sky. Learn how the observatory scans the sky and collects information across different infrared wavelengths.

How did galaxies form and evolve throughout cosmic history? Explore how SPHEREx studies hundreds of millions of galaxies to help scientists investigate the history of the universe.

Water, carbon dioxide, and other molecules are important ingredients in the formation of planetary systems. Discover how SPHEREx investigates these molecules in interstellar clouds and star-forming regions.
