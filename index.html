The Mission

A New Way to See SPHEREx Mission

01

SPHEREx is NASA’s all-sky infrared survey mission that maps the entire sky across 102 infrared colors. Our platform transforms these scientific observations into an interactive virtual telescope, allowing users to explore the infrared universe from anywhere. Users can navigate the sky, search for celestial objects, switch between infrared wavelengths, and examine stars, galaxies, nebulae, and other cosmic phenomena. By making complex astronomical data visual, interactive, and accessible, the platform helps students, researchers, and astronomy enthusiasts understand the hidden features of spherex mission and discover objects that cannot be easily seen using visible light.
