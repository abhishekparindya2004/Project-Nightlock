THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE DISCOVERIES

Understanding the Universe Through Infrared Astronomy

Astronomical observations allow scientists to investigate objects and phenomena far beyond our immediate surroundings. SPHEREx takes this exploration further by collecting spectral information across 102 near-infrared bands.

From distant galaxies to interstellar clouds, its observations provide valuable clues about cosmic evolution, galaxy formation and the distribution of molecules associated with the origins of planetary systems. NightLock brings these scientific concepts together through visual exploration and accessible educational resources.
